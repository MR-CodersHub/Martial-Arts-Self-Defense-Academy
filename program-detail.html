A TYPICAL TRAINING SESSION
ANATOMY OF A KENSEI CLASS

Every session follows an intentional, sports-science backed sequence to ensure peak learning, safety, and progressive conditioning.

Stage 01
10 – 15 Mins

WARM-UP & MOBILITY

Dynamic joint opening, core temperature elevation, rotational stretching, and sport-specific footwork.

Stage 02
20 Mins

TECHNIQUE PRACTICE

Master coach breakdowns of kinetic angles, precision strikes, grappling mechanics, or traditional Kata/Forms.

Stage 03
15 Mins

PARTNER DRILLS

Controlled pad work, focus mitts, clinch work, or positional mat flow under safe cooperative guidance.

Stage 04
10 – 15 Mins

CONTROLLED SPARRING

Live situational application, positional rolling, or light technical sparring tailored strictly to student skill tier.

Stage 05
5 Mins

COOL-DOWN & RECOVERY

Parasympathetic breathing (Mokuso), static flexibility, bow-out dojo etiquette, and coach Q&A.

Common Questions • Transparent Guidance

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Have questions before stepping onto the tatami? Here are answers to the most common inquiries from new students and parents.

We welcome students starting from age 5 in our Junior Samurai cohort, up to practitioners in their 60s and 70s. All classes are stratified by age group and skill level so training is always safe and age-appropriate.

No prior martial arts experience or baseline fitness is required! Over 65% of our new members start with zero combat sports background. Our instructors guide you step-by-step through fundamental stances and movements at your own comfortable pace.

For your free trial, simply wear comfortable athletic clothing (t-shirt and sweatpants or shorts). All required protective gear (loaner Gi, boxing gloves, shin guards) is sanitized and provided free of charge by the academy.

Standard classes run for 60 to 75 minutes. We maintain a strict maximum 12:1 student-to-instructor ratio on the mats to ensure individualized postural feedback, safety oversight, and rapid skill retention.

You will be greeted by our admissions coordinator, given a tour of our hospital-grade tatami facility, fitted with any loaner equipment, and paired with an experienced assistant coach who walks you through the fundamentals. There is zero pressure and zero commitment.
