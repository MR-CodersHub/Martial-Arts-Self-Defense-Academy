TERMS & CONDITIONS
ACADEMY ENROLLMENT STANDARDS

Please read these terms carefully. By registering for classes, purchasing a membership, or stepping onto the KENSEI tatami, you agree to these academy bylaws.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Document Version: 3.4 Jurisdiction: State of New York, USA

01 Membership & Enrollment Terms

Enrollment at KENSEI grants access to scheduled group training sessions corresponding to your selected tier (Fundamental, Unlimited Combat, or Master Faculty). Memberships are personal, non-transferable, and require an active digital profile with verified emergency contact information.

  • Tuition is billed on a monthly autopay cycle via encrypted credit card or ACH transfer.
  • 30-day written cancellation notice is required prior to the next billing date for standard monthly agreements.
  • Members may request a medical freeze for up to 90 consecutive days with a qualified physician’s note.

02 Code of Non-Violence & Defensive Ethos

Martial arts techniques taught at KENSEI—including striking, joint locks, throws, and chokes—are strictly for character cultivation, athletic competition, and ethical self-preservation.

  • Zero-Street Violence Policy: Unprovoked aggression, street brawling, bullying, or harassment outside the academy results in immediate expulsion with forfeiture of tuition.
  • Training skills may only be practiced under qualified instructor supervision on approved mats.
  • Students must uphold the code of honor and humility in all public interactions.

03 Assumption of Inherent Risk & Waiver

Martial arts, combat sports, and tactical conditioning involve physical contact and dynamic movement that carry inherent physiological risks, including sprains, contusions, and strains.

  • Every participant executes an Assumption of Risk & Liability Waiver prior to stepping onto the tatami.
  • Students are required to disclose pre-existing medical conditions, cardiovascular history, and past surgeries.
  • KENSEI maintains AED equipment, first responder staff, and hospital-grade sanitization protocols to maximize training safety.

04 Tatami Hygiene & Uniform Standards

Maintaining hospital-grade cleanliness protects our entire community from staph, ringworm, and bacterial spread. Compliance with hygiene rules is mandatory.

  • All uniforms (Gi, rashguards, shorts) must be laundered and completely dry before each session.
  • Finger and toe nails must be cleanly trimmed short to prevent cuts and accidental eye injuries.
  • Jewelry, watches, earrings, and metal piercings must be removed prior to stepping onto the mat.

PRIVACY POLICY
TRANSPARENT DATA ETHICS

KENSEI respects your digital privacy. We collect only what is strictly necessary to train you safely, process your membership, and communicate essential schedule alerts.

Compliance: GDPR • CCPA • NY SHIELD Act Encryption: 256-Bit SSL / TLS End-to-End Third-Party Policy: Zero Sale of Student Data

01 Information We Collect

When you register for a complimentary trial pass, enroll in a program, or use our mobile schedule portal, we may collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Full legal name, date of birth, email, phone number, and home address.
  • Emergency Contact: Name, relationship, and telephone number of designated emergency contacts.
  • Health & Physical Disclosures: Known injuries, physical limitations, or cardiovascular notes to assist instructors in modifying drills.
  • Payment Data: Tokenized payment credentials securely handled via certified PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processors (e.g., Stripe). KENSEI never stores raw credit card numbers.

02 Dojo Cameras & Media Consent

For the security of our students and the analytical breakdown of high-level martial arts technique, KENSEI operates facility cameras:

  • 24/7 Security CCTV: Active in training areas, pro shop, and entryways to ensure student safety. Never placed in locker rooms or restrooms.
  • Technical Coaching Video: Instructors occasionally record sparring or drill sessions for biomechanical technique review with students.
  • Promotional Media Opt-Out: Photos and videos taken for academy marketing require student release. Students may request a complete media opt-out at any time at the front desk.

03 How We Protect Your Information

We deploy robust technological safeguards to protect personal data from unauthorized access, breach, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encrypted data transmission using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Strict role-based access control limiting student file access only to authorized senior staff and certified coaches.
  • Regular vulnerability scanning and security audits across all digital attendance platforms.

04 Your Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full authority over your personal information under applicable state and international data privacy statutes:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request an export of your training records, attendance history, and profile data.
  • Right to Rectification: Update inaccurate contact details or emergency contacts at any time.
  • Right to Deletion: Upon membership termination, you may request permanent deletion of your profile data by emailing privacy@kenseiacademy.com.

Tatami Etiquette • Life-First Code

THE DOJO SAFETY CODE
6 PILLARS OF RESPECTFUL COMBAT

Our mats are sacred ground built on mutual trust, mutual growth, and uncompromised safety. Every student and instructor abides by these six unbreakable rules.

1. Tap Early • Tap Often

Ego has zero place on our tatami. When trapped in a submission, tap your partner, tap the mat, or shout "TAP!" clearly. When your partner taps, release pressure immediately. We train to return tomorrow stronger.

2. Hospital-Grade Hygiene

Clean body, clean gear. Rashguards, Gi uniforms, hand wraps, and shin guards must be freshly washed prior to every class. Open wounds must be cleanly bandaged and taped before stepping onto the mat.

3. Strict Footwear Segregation

Never wear outdoor shoes onto the tatami—ever. Conversely, never walk barefoot off the mat into hallways, restrooms, or locker rooms. Always step into sandals or slides the second you step off the mat.

4. Calibrated Sparring Pace

Calibrate your sparring intensity to match your training partner's skill level, weight, and experience. Technical control, fluid movement, and defensive awareness always take precedence over blunt force.

5. Trimmed Nails & No Jewelry

Keep finger and toe nails neatly trimmed and filed short to prevent scratches and accidental eye gouges. Remove all metal piercings, watches, necklaces, and rings before stepping on the tatami.

6. Mutual Respect & Bowing

Bow upon stepping onto and off the tatami. Acknowledge your instructors and training partners with a respectful bow (rei) before and after every drill. Support each other's journey with humility and encouragement.
