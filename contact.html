Admissions FAQ • Beginner Guidance

FREQUENTLY ASKED
ENROLLMENT QUESTIONS

Clear, transparent answers to common inquiries regarding trial sessions, memberships, and academy culture.

Have Another Question?

Your free trial pass grants you full access to any beginner or fundamentals class on our timetable. Arrive 15 minutes before scheduled start time; an instructor will welcome you, show you through our locker rooms, introduce you to the senior student mentor who will partner with you, and guide you through the warm-up, drills, and cool-down at your own comfortable pace. There is zero pressure to spar.

No. Over 70% of our members started with zero martial arts experience and varying fitness levels. Training is designed precisely to build core strength, mobility, and cardiovascular stamina progressively over your first 60 days. You will never be asked to perform beyond your current physical capabilities.

Wear standard comfortable athletic clothing (t-shirt and shorts/joggers). We train barefoot on sanitized Olympic Tatami. We provide freshly sanitized loaner boxing gloves, shin guards, and Gis for trial students completely free of charge. Just bring a water bottle and an open mind.

We offer structured age cohorts: Junior Warriors (ages 5 to 12), Teens Combatives (ages 13 to 17), and Adult Cohorts (ages 18 to 65+). There is no upper age limit for adults; our oldest active black belt student is 71 years old!

We operate with total pricing transparency. We offer flexible month-to-month memberships with no cancellation fees, as well as discounted 6-month and 12-month developmental commitments for students aiming for rank advancement. All plans include access to open mat times and locker amenities.

Yes. Our Hybrid Warrior membership grants unlimited cross-training across all 7 disciplines (e.g. Karate on Monday, BJJ on Wednesday, Muay Thai on Friday). You are free to explore and combine traditions as your skills evolve.
