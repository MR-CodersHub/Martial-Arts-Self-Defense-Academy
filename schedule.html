Dojo Protocols • Student Expectations

IMPORTANT SCHEDULE INFORMATION
ATTENDANCE & DOJO ETIQUETTE

Clear standards ensure every training session is safe, organized, respectful, and productive for all practitioners.

CLASS DURATION & ARRIVAL PROTOCOL

Standard classes run between 60 to 75 minutes. Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes prior to class to change, wrap hands, and warm up your joints before the formal opening bow.

PUNCTUALITY & TATAMI ENTRY

If arriving after the opening bow, please stand respectfully at the perimeter edge of the tatami mat and wait for the instructor’s nod before bowing and stepping onto the training floor.

ATTENDANCE FOR BELT GRADINGS

Progression through KENSEI belt ranks requires consistent mat presence. Students must log a minimum attendance record (typically 2 sessions/week over 3 to 6 months) to qualify for grading invitations.

EQUIPMENT & HYGIENE STANDARDS

Clean training uniforms (Dogi, Dobok, or rashguards) are mandatory. Finger and toenails must be neatly trimmed. All personal gloves, shin guards, and mouthguards must be sanitized before stepping on the tatami.

HOLIDAY SCHEDULES & WEATHER UPDATES

Dojo closures or holiday timetables are broadcasted via our Member WhatsApp channel and posted on this website 48 hours in advance. Emergency weather closures are announced by 06:00 AM on the day of training.
